## Curation Notes

- The curated KB paraphrases and summarizes the source documents for chat use; it is not a verbatim copy of the PDFs/PPTX.
- The original source files were not copied into the repository.
- The 2024 schedule/deadline document is intentionally not treated as current semester guidance.
- The KB preserves assistant boundaries: no full assignment writing, no grading claims, no invented policies, and no current deadline claims without Moodle/lecturer confirmation.

## Review Workflow

Before hosted pilot use, the lecturer/content owner should review:

- Assessment weights and module summaries.
- AI-use guidance and disclosure language.
- Reflective writing and e-portfolio support wording.
- Historical deadline warning.
- Escalation guidance for lecturer and LMS administrator support.
